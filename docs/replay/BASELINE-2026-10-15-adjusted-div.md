Excess = a stock's forward return minus the median Nifty 500 stock's over the same window (index history before Dec 2025 is not stored, so differences between groups are the readable part, not absolute levels). Observations overlap and cluster in time; no significance test. Delisted stocks drop out of the forward returns. ATR is a close-to-close proxy. Closes are back-adjusted for 791 actions (plain splits and bonuses confirmed by the stored prices, cash dividends of 5% of price or more, rights by the ex-rights price); the guard now covers only actions that cannot be adjusted. Guard removed 3157 (20 sessions) and 3431 (60 sessions) observations; 426022 and 404667 kept. Parity with src/pm/verdict.ts at 2026-10-15: 2917 of 2917 stocks identical.
