- Two books exactly as already tested after the split fix: growth combo (50 names x 2%, 35% trail, cost 2.0%, no gate) and trend leaders (50 names x 2%, 35% trail, cost 1.3%), signals 2011–2023, marked daily.
- Blend: 50% of capital in each, rebalanced daily (ignores rebalancing cost, so it flatters the blend slightly). Reported for the whole span and for the 2018-01 start (equity reset), with each book alone beside it, the correlation of their monthly returns, and the Nifty 500.
- Bar (same as before): CAGR above the Nifty 500 in both starts and worst drawdown at most 35%. Also reported: the drawdown of the blend against the worse of the two books.
- Disclosure: both books were already run on this span, so this combines two evaluated results and is a weaker test than a fresh sample. A pass makes the case for a combined paper book, not for live money.
