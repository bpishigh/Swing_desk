# QUIET-COMPOUNDERS: stocks that compound steadily — registered 20 Nov 2026, before any run
Owner thread: the "steady" type of multibagger (HUNT, anatomy: 27% of India winners launched without a crash, calm, shallow drawdown). Question: what do stocks that compound smoothly over three years have in common beforehand, judged so that volatility cannot flatter the result. Paper only; not advice.
## Outcome (the only one)
S: at a month-end t, the close at t+756 sessions is at least 2.5x the close at t (about 36% a year) AND the worst fall from a running high of the closes in (t, t+756] is at most 35%. Both conditions use returns only (no price level).
## Data
India as in the other studies (liquid group: 20-session traded value >= Rs 2 crore, raw price >= Rs 20, all listed names including later delisted). Month-ends 2011-02 to 2023-11 (needs 756 forward sessions), 504 sessions of history where a feature needs it.
## Set P (price and volume features, no fundamentals; selection 2011-02..2017-12, scored 2018-01..2023-11)
mom6 (126-session return), mom12_1 (252 return skipping 21), vol126 (daily volatility, low side tested), dd2y (close / 2-year high - 1), prox52 (close / 252-day high), logTV, rawPrice (raw close), rs6 (stock 126-session return minus Nifty 500's), smooth (R-squared of ln(close) on time over 252 sessions), upShare (share of the last twelve 21-session blocks that closed up), tvTrend (20-session value / value 125-250 sessions ago).
## Set F (adds fundamentals as of the month-end: revenue growth, pre-exceptional margin, EBIT margin, interest cover, earnings yield, sales yield; month-ends 2019-01..2023-11; selection 2019-01..2021-06, scored 2021-07..2023-11); Set P features are repeated on this window.
## Selection and bar (per set, selection period only for choosing, scored period once)
A feature's top or bottom fifth (side with the higher lift) is kept if lift in S >= 1.5 within the group, >= 1.2 in each of the three traded-value terciles (so it is not just a size effect), and the median 12-month return of that fifth is above the group median. At most three kept (largest smaller-tercile lift first). The frozen combination (selection-period cuts) passes if on the scored period n >= 150, S-rate lift >= 2.0 against the group rate, median 12-month excess > 0 and mean 756-session return above the group's, and at least half the calendar years have a positive median excess. No portfolio replay in this step.
