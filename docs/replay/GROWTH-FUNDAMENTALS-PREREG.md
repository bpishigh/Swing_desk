# Growth factors with quarterly results — registered 18 Nov 2026, before any run

Correction first: earlier today I wrote that India has no earnings history to backtest. That was wrong. `data/india/filings` holds quarterly results from the NSE filing list for periods 2016-06 to 2026-09, each with its filing timestamp, so a point-in-time test is possible from the 2017-06 quarter (year-on-year needs a year of history). Paper only; not financial advice.

## Setup (same as docs/replay/GROWTH-FACTORS-PREREG.md except where stated)
- Universe, outcomes (3x, 5x within 756 sessions; 12-month forward return), month-end quintiles and the 13 price/volume factors are as before.
- Five results factors added (family now 18; delivery % still excluded): revenue growth year on year in the latest quarter; profit after tax growth year on year (only where the year-ago profit is positive); revenue growth acceleration (this quarter's growth minus the previous quarter's growth); change in pre-tax margin against the year-ago quarter; profit growth acceleration (both quarters with positive bases).
- Point in time: a quarter counts only if its filing timestamp is on or before the month-end. Consolidated figures if filed by then, otherwise standalone. The latest quarter must be at most 150 days old at the month-end. Original filings are used, not later revisions.
- Samples: discovery month-ends 2018-01 to 2020-12 (36 months); validation month-ends 2021-01 to 2023-11 (35 months), opened once. Only one regime pair is available, so base rates are high in validation (broad small-cap boom) and lift is judged against the same-period all-stock rate.

## Reading rule (no amendments after seeing results)
- A factor is useful if its favourable quintile has a 3x rate at least 1.5x the all-stock rate and the opposite quintile at most 1.0x, and the favourable direction holds in at least 2 of the 3 discovery years.
- Carry at most the 3 useful factors with the largest lift into one combined rule (favourable quintile of all three on the month-end). Discovery bar: lift at least 2x with at least 150 stock-months. If fewer than 2 factors are useful, nothing is carried and the study closes.
- Validation (once): lift at least 1.5x, 12-month median above the all-stock median by at least 5 points, at least 150 stock-months, lift above 1 in at least 2 of 3 validation years.
- Pass leads to a registered portfolio replay on the same bar as before (beat Nifty 500 CAGR, drawdown at most 35%); fail closes results-based factors on this data.

## Addendum 1 (18 Nov 2026): correction found on the first discovery table (same run, recomputed)
The first discovery table divided each factor's quintile rates by the all-stock 3x rate (23.5%). The results factors only exist for stocks with filings (covered stocks), whose 3x rate is much higher, so those lifts were inflated by coverage, not by the factor. Fix, applied to every factor alike: lift is the quintile rate divided by the 3x rate among the stocks that have that factor on those month-ends. The filing list also holds only companies listed today, so covered stocks are survivors; this biases the level of every rate upward but not the ranking inside the covered group, and the result has to be read with that in mind. No threshold changed.

Discovery result with the corrected lifts (docs/replay/GROWTH-FACTORS-fund-discovery.md, month-ends 2018-01 to 2020-12; only about a third of stock-months have results data, and 2018 has almost none because year-on-year needs a year of history): revenue growth top quintile 0.95x, profit growth 1.04x, revenue acceleration 0.99x, margin change 0.99x, profit acceleration 1.00x. The lowest-growth quintile does better (1.12x to 1.25x), the 2018–2020 pattern of cheap names rebounding after the 2020 crash. No results factor reaches the 1.5x bar, so nothing is carried and the 2021–2023 window stays closed. In the same window momentum is weak (6-month return 1.06x) while low price is the strongest price factor (1.63x for the lowest quintile).
Verdict: month-end quintiles on results growth do not find growth stocks in this data. This does not test results timing (the days around the filing), which the next study does.

## Addendum 2 (18 Nov 2026): bug found in the study scripts, results below supersede earlier numbers where marked
The studies in this series (trend leaders C-234 to C-237, IPO winners C-232/C-233, growth factors portfolio C-240/C-241, and the first run of the results-event study) took entry and exit prices from the raw opens but followed price moves on split-adjusted closes. A stock that split or issued bonus shares while held showed a false crash of 50% or more, biasing every result downward. Fix: opens are put on the adjusted scale (raw open times adjusted close over raw close of the same day). Rules, samples and bars are unchanged; the old runs are voided and the numbers are re-run once each; files carry the suffix -adj. The factor tables (C-238, C-239, C-242) use closes only and are not affected. The earlier IPO base replay (C-226 to C-231) used the engine's own fill code and is checked separately.
