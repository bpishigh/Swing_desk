Generated by `scripts/replay-verdicts.mjs` from the repo's stored prices (2021-04-01 to 2026-10-15, 1367 sessions). Start dates every 5 sessions from 2022-02-07. Excess = a stock's forward return minus the median Nifty 500 stock's over the same window (index history before Dec 2025 is not stored, so differences between groups are the readable part, not absolute levels). Observations overlap and cluster in time; no significance test. Delisted stocks drop out of the forward returns. ATR is a close-to-close proxy. Closes are back-adjusted for 789 actions (plain splits and bonuses confirmed by the stored prices, cash dividends of 5% of price or more, rights by the ex-rights price); the guard now covers only actions that cannot be adjusted. Guard removed 522 (20 sessions) and 566 (60 sessions) observations; 426792 and 405695 kept. Parity with src/pm/verdict.ts at 2026-10-15: 2917 of 2917 stocks identical.
