# Replay of the Legacy exit test on stored closes

Generated by `scripts/replay-verdicts.mjs` from the repo's stored prices (2021-04-01 to 2026-10-14, 1366 sessions). Start dates every 5 sessions from 2022-02-07. Excess = a stock's forward return minus the median Nifty 500 stock's over the same window (index history before Dec 2025 is not stored, so differences between groups are the readable part, not absolute levels). Observations overlap and cluster in time; no significance test. Delisted stocks drop out of the forward returns. ATR is a close-to-close proxy. Closes are back-adjusted for 524 plain splits and bonuses confirmed by the stored prices; the guard now covers only actions that cannot be adjusted. Guard removed 3226 (20 sessions) and 3503 (60 sessions) observations; 425953 and 404595 kept. Parity with src/pm/verdict.ts at 2026-10-14: 2914 of 2914 stocks identical.

## Pre-registered tests (Engine Rules C-42, C-43)

- **C-42, 200 only: near (within 1 ATR):** FAIL (exit at the first close); full sample fails, 1 of 4 years pass.
- **C-42, 200 only: mid (1 ATR to 3%):** PASS (wait for confirmation); full sample passes, 3 of 4 years pass.
- **C-42, 200 only: far (beyond both):** FAIL (exit at the first close); full sample fails, 1 of 4 years pass.
- **C-43, deep swing low above the 200-day:** FAIL (swing leg above the 200-day is context only); full sample fails, 1 of 4 years pass, Red-only same sign.

A calendar year counts only with at least 100 observations in the cell (implementation detail added when the script was written).
