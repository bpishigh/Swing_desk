# INSIDER-WINNERS: which stocks with promoter / insider buying grew exponentially, and what do they share? — registered 21 Nov 2026, before the run
Owner: "see which stocks with high promoter and insider buying grew exponentially and draw insights and strat". Same method as the multibagger anatomy: winners first, then common features, then one rule tested on an untouched half. Paper only; not financial advice. Caveat carried from INSIDER: the plain signal failed its bar and its holdout (docs/replay/INSIDER-PREREG.md); this asks whether a subset of buying events is where the winners are.
## Events
Stock-months (month-end 2016-06 to 2023-11, 756-session outcome complete) where a promoter / promoter-group net open-market purchase >= Rs 25 lakh, or a director / KMP net purchase >= Rs 10 lakh, was disclosed in the trailing 90 days (P1 or P3 of INSIDER), liquid at Rs 2 crore average daily traded value and raw price >= Rs 20. Exponential = highest close within 756 sessions >= 3x the month-end close (5x also reported). Episode view for naming winners: the first such month-end for a stock after 6 months without an event.
## Part 1 (description, no test): the winners
Count of winner episodes and stock-months by year, by industry (current classification, coverage reported), the top 25 winners by multiple (name, month, multiple, size of buying), share of winners from 2020 launches.
## Part 2 (features at the event; fixed list of 12)
Net buy value over 20-day traded value (buying relative to liquidity); number of separate buy disclosures; promoter share of the buying; promoter holding before the first buy (%); holding gain (points); 6-month return; 12-month return; distance below 252-day high; drawdown from 2-year high; 126-day volatility; log traded value; raw price; gain above the 252-day low; above the 200-day mean (yes/no).
Method: quintile cuts set on the discovery events (month-ends 2016-06 to 2020-12). Lift = 3x rate in the quintile over the event base rate. A feature is useful if the favourable end has lift >= 1.4 in discovery, the opposite end <= 1.0, and the direction holds in at least 4 of 5 discovery years with >= 20 events. Validation (2021-01 to 2023-11, one look): the same cuts (discovery thresholds) must keep lift >= 1.2 and the 12-month median excess of the favourable side above that of the rest.
## Part 3 (rule)
Take at most the two useful features with the largest discovery lift; the rule is "event and favourable end of both". Discovery bar: >= 150 events, 3x lift >= 1.8 against all events. Validation bar (one run, 2021-01 to 2023-11): >= 150 events, 3x lift >= 1.5 over all events, median 12-month excess >= +8 points over the liquid median on the month-end, positive in at least 2 of 3 years, and ex-top-5% excess > 0. A pass goes to a forward paper book flagged "found after a failed plain signal; thin evidence". A fail ends it.

## Results (one run; the first launch stopped on a duplicate variable before any output, fixed, then run once; raw output docs/replay/INSIDER-WINNERS-output.txt)
4,489 buying stock-months (972 episodes); 3x rate 24.7% (22.2% discovery, 28.3% validation); 5x 9.6%; 260 winner episodes.
Part 1: winners follow the market, not the insider. 3x rate by entry year: 2016 6.0%, 2017 6.6%, 2018 12.2%, 2019 24.9%, 2020 46.5%, 2021 32.5%, 2022-23 25.8%. 88 of 260 winner episodes entered in Mar-Dec 2020. Industry: capital goods (48% rate), textiles and realty (39%), IT (33%), chemicals (31%) over-represent; healthcare and unclassified under-represent. Of the top 25, 22 were promoter-led buying, 17 entered at or after a crash-trough or deep drawdown from the 2-year high (-20% to -60%), and 8 were strong-momentum names buying near a new high (TANLA, AURIONPRO, GRAVITA, GOODLUCK, RKFORGE, WEBELSOLAR); repeat buyers in the same stock (APARINDS, BLS, ANANTRAJ, AURIONPRO) appear twice. Typical buying was small (0.1-0.9 days of volume).
Part 2: the size of the buying does not matter (net buy over traded value: lifts 0.7-1.1, no pattern; number of buys: weak; promoter share and holding level: nothing). What predicts a 3x in the events is the stock's state: low price (lift 1.56 in discovery, 1.51 validation, all 5 years), high volatility (1.93 / 1.28, 4 of 5 years), deep 2-year drawdown (1.96, did not hold in validation), weak 12-month momentum (1.91, did not hold).
Part 3 (rule as registered: the two largest discovery lifts = deep 2-year drawdown and high volatility): discovery n 242, 3x 49.6% (lift 2.23), median excess +16.5%; validation n 27 (needs 150), 3x 11.1% (lift 0.39), median excess -28.4%. FAIL. The rule only existed in the 2020 trough: it selects crash troughs, and there were almost none in 2021-23.
Conclusion: promoter or insider buying adds little beyond what low-priced, volatile, beaten-down stocks already do; winners among them come from crash recoveries (2020) and a few strong-momentum promoter-led names. The one consistent, non-crash feature is low price (small stocks). No rule, no book. Interesting leftovers, not tested: promoter buying while the stock is near a new high (8 of the top 25) and repeat buying in the same stock.
