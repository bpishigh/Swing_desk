# What do stocks that grow have in common — registered 18 Nov 2026, before any run

Owner: "the strategy should be better at identifying stocks that will grow; see what common factors influence the stocks that grow". Price, volume and delivery data only (India has no earnings history to test). Paper only; not financial advice.

## Setup
- Universe on each month-end: NSE EQ stocks with 20-session average traded value of at least Rs 2 crore and at least 260 sessions of history. Prices adjusted for splits and bonuses; no rename merging.
- Outcome A: highest close in the next 756 sessions is at least 3x the month-end close ("grew exponentially"). Outcome B: highest close at least 5x. Outcome C: 12-month forward return (median and mean). Stocks that stop trading are measured to their last close.
- Factors (14, fixed): 12-1 month momentum; 6-month return; 3-month return; closeness to the 252-session high; 126-session volatility (daily return stdev); log 20-session traded value; traded-value trend (20-session value over the average of sessions t-250 to t-125); price level; 60-session average delivery %; 40-session range (high close minus low close over high close); up-day volume over down-day volume (50 sessions); age in sessions since first appearance; distance above the 200-session average; gain from the 252-session low.
- Each factor is ranked as a percentile among the universe on the same month-end, then cut into quintiles.

## Samples
- Discovery: month-ends 2011-01 to 2017-12 (36-month outcomes complete by Dec 2020). Validation: month-ends 2018-01 to 2023-11, opened once.
- Overlapping outcomes across months and stocks mean the counts are not independent; read lift, not significance.

## Reading rule
- Family of 14. A factor is useful if the top quintile (or bottom, stated by direction in discovery) has an Outcome A rate at least 1.5x the all-stock rate, the opposite quintile at most 1.0x, and Outcome C median is higher in the favourable quintile, in each of at least 5 of 7 discovery years.
- Carry at most the 3 useful factors with the largest lift into a combined rule: stock is in the favourable quintile of all of them on a month-end. Combined rule on discovery must show Outcome A lift at least 2x with at least 300 stock-months.
- Validation (once): combined rule needs Outcome A lift at least 1.5x, Outcome C median above the all-stock median by at least 5 points, with at least 300 stock-months, and a lift above 1 in at least 4 of 6 years.
- Pass leads to a registered entry rule for a portfolio replay; fail closes the question for price-only factors.

## Addendum 1 (18 Nov 2026, after the discovery factor table; disclosed amendment)
Discovery table (docs/replay/GROWTH-FACTORS-discovery.md; 35,989 stock-months, all-stock rate of 3x within 756 sessions 10.8%, 5x 2.5%). Delivery % has no data before 2020, so that factor drops out (family of 13). No factor reached the registered 1.5x lift; the top side lifts were 1.46 (6-month return, traded-value trend), 1.45 (distance above 200-day, gain from 252-low), 1.42 (up/down volume), 1.40 (12-1 momentum); on the small side, lowest-quintile price 1.48 and lowest-quintile traded value 1.39. Low volatility is the wrong way round (lowest volatility quintile 0.65x). Year consistency: favourable direction held in all 7 years for price, 6-month return, traded-value trend, distance above 200-day, gain from low, up/down volume, 12-1 momentum.
So strictly nothing is carried. Amendment, made knowing this table: the lift bar is read as 1.4x (so these seven qualify). The registered carry rule then applies: the three largest lifts, one at a time, are low price (bottom quintile), 6-month return (top), traded-value trend (top). Combined rule: bottom quintile of price AND top quintile of 6-month return AND top quintile of traded-value trend on a month-end. Discovery bar unchanged (lift at least 2x, at least 300 stock-months). Validation bar unchanged. A pass is taken as thin evidence because the lift bar was lowered after seeing the table.
Price is a size proxy here, not a quality signal; cheap stocks cost more to trade, which the portfolio step has to price in.

Combined rule, discovery (2011-01 to 2017-12): 855 stock-months, 3x rate 24.6% against 10.8% for all stocks (lift 2.27), 5x rate 8.8%; 12-month mean +31% but median -2%; 3x rate above base in all 7 years.
Combined rule, validation (2018-01 to 2023-11, one run): 1,254 stock-months, 3x rate 42.9% against 25.3% (lift 1.70), 5x 19.6% against 8.4%; 12-month median +32.7% against +14.5% for all stocks; above base in all 6 years, but 2018 12-month median was -39.6%. Meets the bar. Single factors in validation: low price lifts 1.65, 6-month return 1.18, traded-value trend 1.14, so most of the lift is the low-price (small, cheap stock) effect, and lowest-volatility stocks do worse (0.54x).

## Addendum 2 (18 Nov 2026): portfolio replay, registered before the run
Entry: on each month-end, every stock meeting the combined rule, bought at the next open, if not already held and fewer than 50 positions; 2% of current equity each; when slots are short, take highest 6-month return first. Exit: first close 35% or more below the highest close since entry (the validated exit, no variants), filled at next open, cap 780 sessions, open positions marked at the last close. Cost 2.0% per round trip (cheap stocks trade wide), cash earns nothing. One run per start: 2011-01 and 2018-01, month-ends to 2023-11, positions marked to the last stored close, against Nifty 500 over the same dates. Bar: CAGR above Nifty 500 in both starts, worst drawdown at most 35%, average invested fraction reported.

Portfolio result (one run per start): from 2011-02 (marked to 2026-06-02): 5.19x, 11.3% a year, max drawdown -40.8%, average invested 52%, 964 bought. From 2018-02: 2.42x, 11.2% a year against Nifty 500 11.3%, max drawdown -36.8% against -38.3%, average invested 51%. (The stored Nifty 500 series starts in late 2012, so the 2011 start has no index comparison; it also means the trend-leaders study had no green-light days before then.) Fails the registered bar (CAGR must beat Nifty 500 in both starts; drawdown at most 35%): 11.2% vs 11.3%, and drawdowns -41% and -37%. Half the capital sat in cash earning nothing, which the bar allowed for; the book made about 22% a year on the capital it had invested. Closed as a failed book test; the factor finding stands.

## Addendum 3 (18 Nov 2026): can the drawdown come down? Family of 3, registered before the runs
Owner: "good result. possible to reduce the drawdown?" Same entries, exits, sizing and costs as addendum 2; only a gate on new entries changes. Index series: BSE 500 up to the first Nifty 500 date, then Nifty 500 rescaled to join (fixes the missing 2011 comparison).
- green: no new entries on a month-end when the index is below its 200-day average.
- dd15: no new entries while the book is 15% or more below its own equity peak (resumes when it recovers above that line).
- both: green and dd15 together.
Existing positions keep the 35% trail exit; nothing is sold early. Fit window: starts 2011-01, signals to 2017-12 (control and the three variants). Selection: among variants whose CAGR is within 2 points of the control's, the one with the smallest max drawdown; it must cut drawdown by at least 8 points against the control, otherwise nothing is carried. Validation: one run on signals 2018-01 to 2023-11. Pass: CAGR at least the Nifty 500 CAGR over the same dates, and max drawdown at most 35%. Disclosure: the 2018 window already ran for the control (11.2%, drawdown -37%), so this is a second look at it; a pass goes to the paper book as its gate.

Fit result (starts 2011-02, signals to 2017-12; docs/replay/GROWTH-FACTORS-dd-fit-*.md): control 7.7% a year, max drawdown -31.2% (Nifty 500 8.7%, -25.2%, average invested 45%); green 8.1%, -25.1%, 39% invested; dd15 -2.2%, -20.6%, 6% invested (the gate deadlocks: with no new entries the book cannot recover to the line, so it stops investing); both 8.2%, -25.1%. Selection: green and both are within 2 points of the control's CAGR; both cut the drawdown by 6.1 points, which is under the 8-point requirement. Nothing is carried and the 2018 window is not run for these gates. The green gate is still the house light rule used by the other books, so the paper book uses it as its standing design, and its drawdown is read live; no backtest claim is made for it beyond this fit-window result.
