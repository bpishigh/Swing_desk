# US multibagger anatomy — registered 19 Nov 2026, before any run
Owner: "look at the stocks which became multibaggers over the years and see what is common to them ... I need something to be figured out for us". Survivors-only US store (data/us/hist, 2,310 names, 2016-10 on) is accepted by the owner; rates are inflated, so everything is read as lift or as winners against non-winners. Paper only; not advice.

## Part 1 — anatomy of the winners (descriptive, no selection)
Winner = a stock whose adjusted close rose at least 4x from a trough to a later peak within 1,008 sessions (one best event per stock; trough price >= $1, 20-session traded value at the trough >= $1m; stocks with any one-day move beyond 80% dropped as data errors). Also 10x subset. Reported: count, the year the trough fell, size and price at the trough, drawdown from the prior 2-year high into the trough, 126-session volatility and 252-session return at the trough, sessions trough-to-peak, and the same market-cap/price/volatility percentile ranks against all stocks on the trough date; for the names with SEC facts (744 tickers, larger names, disclosed as a biased subset): SIC group, revenue growth year on year, acceleration, profitability, margin change as of the trough and as of the first breakout. Plus the winners' first breakout: first close at a 252-day high after the trough, how many sessions after the trough, what share of the total trough-to-peak move had already happened, volume ratio that day.

## Part 2 — can the first breakout be told apart (tests, registered)
Event E: a close at a 252-day high, at least 1.5x the lowest close of the prior 252 sessions, with no 252-day-high close in the previous 60 sessions (first breakout from a base), 20-day traded value >= $2m, price >= $1; entry at the next session's close (no opens), 35% trailing stop on closes, 504-session cap, cost 0.4% round trip. Outcome A: close at least 2x the entry within 504 sessions. Also the net trade return minus SPY over the same dates, and the 12-month median.
Samples: events 2017-10..2020-12 (first, chosen on) and 2021-01..2024-09 (second, scored on), both with full outcome windows. Features (all at the event close): price, logTV, fromLow, drawdown into the low (low / prior 2-year high), vol126, volume ratio (day volume / 50-day mean), mom6, mom12_1, days since the low, tvTrend, base tightness (20-day to 100-day average abs move), for SEC names: revYoY, revAccel, profitable (pat > 0), marginChg.
Selection (first half only): a feature's top or bottom fifth (direction taken from the first half) is kept if its lift in A is >= 1.4 overall, >= 1.15 in every volatility tercile, and its mean excess trade return is above the all-event mean. At most three features, ranked by smaller tercile lift. The combination is frozen and scored once on the second half: pass if n >= 150, lift in A >= 1.5 against the second-half event base rate, mean excess return above the second-half event mean and above 0, and at least half of years positive. No portfolio replay in this step (no opens).
If nothing is kept, that is reported as the result; the next step is then the anatomy tables, not a new test.

## Result (19 Nov 2026; one run, no amendments)
### Part 1 — the winners (890 stocks rose 4x or more trough to peak within 1,008 sessions; 219 rose 10x or more)
- Where they came from: trough dates cluster in market crashes: 2020 415 of 890 (47%), 2022 195 (22%), 2017-18 109, 2023-25 142. Winners are mostly stocks that were crushed in a bear market and came back, not steady compounders.
- State at the trough (median, 4x set; 10x set in brackets): 62% below the 2-year high (70%); 12-month return -48% (-56%); price $13.85 ($7.35), 19th percentile of price (6th); traded value $22m ($14m), 35th percentile (26th); daily volatility 3.7% (4.4%), 74th percentile (89th). Winners are cheap in price, small-to-mid in traded value and volatile; the 10x set is more extreme on every one.
- Time: median 858 sessions trough to peak (868 for 10x).
- First breakout (first 252-day-high close after the trough): median 132 sessions after the trough (112 for 10x); at that point only 38% of the log move was done (32% for 10x), median volume 1.45x its 50-day mean (1.64x). Most of the move comes after the first breakout.
- Fundamentals (257 of 890 have SEC facts, larger names; biased subset): revenue growth at the trough median +9% (interquartile 0 to +25%), 65% profitable; at the first breakout revenue growth +6% with acceleration +1 point, 58% profitable. Winners were not fast growers by revenue at launch. The 10x set (82 names with facts): revenue growth +7%, 45% profitable.
- Industry (SIC, 320 of 890 with facts): technology/instruments (35-38) 86, software and services (73) 56, retail 32, finance 31, mining and energy 30, chemicals/pharma 20. No single industry dominates; tech and software together are 44% of the names with facts.
### Part 2 — telling first breakouts apart (events: first 1,878, second 3,072; base rate of doubling 31.4% and 21.6%)
Kept by the registered rule on the first half: one feature, price (bottom fifth of price at the breakout: lift 1.44 overall, 1.39/1.30/1.24 across volatility terciles, mean excess return +27.0% against +5.2% for all events). Nothing else met the rule (days since the low, volume ratio, tvTrend, mom6, revYoY and profitability were on the right side but missed 1.4x or a tercile). The registered combination is therefore just the price fifth. Scored once on the second half using the first half's price cut: n 664, doubled in 40.4% against 21.6% base (lift 1.87), mean net trade return +30.5%, mean excess +19.6% against +0.8% for all events, 12-month median +14.1% against +4.2%, excess positive in all four years (2021 +7.5, 2022 +63.1, 2023 +20.9, 2024 +15.8). It passes the bar. First half years: 2017 +9.9, 2018 +11.9, 2019 -2.4, 2020 +54.5.
Reading and cautions: (1) price is the one thing a survivors-only store inflates most, because cheap stocks that went to zero are missing; the US lift for low price is an upper bound and cannot be trusted for size. (2) The result leans on crash years (2020, 2022 carry most of the excess); outside them it is +8 to +21 points but with the same survivorship upward bias. (3) The pattern that survives is a crash-rebound: buy the first 252-day-high breakout of a cheap stock after a deep fall. It is the opposite of the India new-high-in-green-market finding in how it is timed. (4) No costs beyond 0.4% and no opens: a direction check only. Next: the same anatomy on India, where the store carries delisted names and the price question can be answered cleanly.

## Addendum IN (registered 19 Nov 2026, before the run): the same anatomy on India
India bhav history covers the whole market including names that later delisted or crashed, so cheap-price results are not survivorship inflated the way the US ones are (companies still in the entities list are not required here). Same definitions with these changes only: trough price >= Rs 20, trough 20-session traded value >= Rs 1 crore (Part 1) and >= Rs 2 crore (events), cost 1.3% round trip, fill at next adjusted open, comparator Nifty 500 (BSE 500 before it starts). No SEC-style facts (India filings list is survivors-only), so no fundamentals in this run. Event samples: first 2011-02..2018-12 (chosen on), second 2019-01..2024-09 (scored on). Selection rule and pass bar unchanged. In India the new-high-in-green-light rules passed (HUNT-1); this run asks whether crash troughs, low price and early breakouts also characterise India winners, and whether first breakout from a deep low beats the plain new-high rules.

### Result IN (19 Nov 2026; one run, output printed twice because the first pipe to `head` cut the output, same code and data, no change)
Part 1, India (460 stocks rose 4x or more within 1,008 sessions; 130 rose 10x or more): trough dates cluster in crashes even more than in the US: 2020 231 of 460 (50%) and 87 of 130 (67%) of the 10x set; 2022 50; 2013 33; 2011 19. At the trough (4x set; 10x in brackets): 52% below the 2-year high (56%), 12-month return -40% (-40%), price Rs 90 (Rs 56) at the 30th percentile of price (22nd), traded value Rs 4.9 crore (Rs 3.2 crore) at the 42nd percentile (29th), volatility at the 43rd percentile (56th) - so Indian winners are cheap and beaten down but NOT unusually volatile, unlike US winners (74th and 89th). First 252-day-high breakout: median 105 sessions after the trough (101), 26% of the log move done (22%), volume 3.05x the 50-day mean (3.01x), which is much stronger than the US 1.45x: an Indian winner's first breakout arrives on heavy volume.
Part 2, India: events first 1,692 (base doubling rate 22.0%), second 3,215 (37.9%). No feature met the registered rule on the first half (best: logTV small 1.31, days-since-low short 1.32, price low 1.28; all missed 1.4 and some tercile cells), so there is no combination to score. The second half, shown for context only: low price 1.29, small size 1.20, short time since low 1.25, 12-1 momentum low 1.24, none decisive. Reading: first breakouts from deep lows in India cannot be told apart by price, size, volume, volatility, tightness or momentum with the features registered; the common factor is the market regime of the launch (a crash trough), not a stock feature. Next test registered below.

## Addendum REGIME (registered 19 Nov 2026, before the run): does the launch regime, not a stock feature, decide who becomes a multibagger?
Month-end cross-sections as in GROWTH-FACTORS (India month-ends 2011-02..2023-11 with the Rs 2 crore floor and the same data checks; US 2017-10..2023-11, $2m floor). Index drawdown at month-end = index close / its 252-session high - 1 (Nifty 500 spliced with BSE 500; SPY). Buckets: deep (<= -20%), middle (-20% to -10%), calm (> -10%). Per bucket: month-ends, separate episodes (runs of consecutive deep month-ends), all-stock 3x-in-756-sessions rate and 12-month median return, and for the sub-group of stocks at least 50% below their 2-year high the same two numbers, plus the sub-group's rate in each deep episode on its own.
Bar (descriptive, no tuning): the regime matters if the beaten-down sub-group's 3x rate in the deep bucket is >= 1.5x its rate in the calm bucket in each market, and the deep-bucket excess shows in at least 3 separate episodes in India and 2 in the US (one crash is not a pattern). Not a strategy test; if it passes, the next step is a registered rule that buys only in deep regimes.

### Result REGIME (19 Nov 2026; US one run; India run twice: the first run required 504 sessions of history and so dropped 2011 and early 2012, the second (disclosed amendment, same data) requires 300 sessions with the 2-year high taken over what exists, to include the 2011-12 decline; the second is the reported one)
US: deep index drawdown (<= -20%) occurred at only 3 month-ends (Mar 2020, Jun 2022, Sep 2022). Beaten-down stocks (>= 50% below 2-year high) became 3x in 37.9% of deep months against 29.5% in calm months (ratio 1.28; bar 1.5); March 2020 alone was 53.5%, the two 2022 month-ends were 26.7% and 29.9%, at or below calm. India: 8 deep month-ends in 4 episodes; the beaten-down sub-group 3x rate was 39.6% deep against 23.0% calm (ratio 1.72), but by episode: Aug-Sep 2011 6.2%, Nov-Dec 2011 10.7%, Feb 2016 16.5%, Mar-May 2020 63.6%. Bar fails in both markets on the episode rule (excess shows in one episode, 2020, in each market). Reading: the clustering of winners in 2020 is one V-shaped rebound, not a repeatable regime rule. A "buy in crashes" rule has one observation behind it; it cannot be validated with this data and I will not build a book on it.
### Winner types (descriptive split of the saved winner sets, no new test)
India (460 4x winners): crash-launch (>= 50% below the 2-year high at the trough) 245 (53%): median -68% drawdown, volatility at the 56th percentile, first breakout 156 sessions after the low with 35% of the move done. No-crash winners (drawdown shallower than 35%) 124 (27%): median -22% drawdown, 12-month return +11%, volatility at the 16th percentile (very calm), price 37th and traded value 38th percentile, first breakout only 34 sessions after the low on 3.3x volume with 13% of the move done. US: crash-launch 617 of 890 (69%), volatility 81st percentile, 48% of the move done at breakout; no-crash 132 (15%), volatility 44th percentile, first breakout 36 sessions after the low. Excluding 2020 troughs the picture is the same but milder (US 475, India 229).

### Result CAPTURE (19 Nov 2026, descriptive; scripts/multibagger-capture.mjs on the India winner set)
How many of the 460 India 4x winners did the HUNT-1 rules flag between trough and peak (green light, new 252-day high, liquidity floor)? M1 93%, M3 91%, M4 34%, any of the three 100% (459 of 460). Median entry came 157 sessions after the trough with 66% of the log move still ahead (M1 62%, M3 53%); for the no-crash type 82 sessions after the trough with 80% still ahead, for the crash type 192 sessions with 57% ahead; 10x winners: flagged 100% by M3, 71% of the move still ahead at the first flag. This is recall only: the rules also fire on thousands of non-winners (HUNT-1: 2,019 M1 events in 2011-18 alone, and a median trade of -6%). The precision is the HUNT-1 result: events beat the index by 10-21 points on average, mostly through the few big winners. It says the rules are not missing multibaggers, the question is which of the events become one.

## Addendum PRECISION (registered 19 Nov 2026, before the run): among the India HUNT-1 events, what separates the ones that become 3x?
Events: M1 or M3 firing as defined in HUNT-1 (green light, close at a 252-day high, with the M1 volume/base conditions or the top-10% 126-session return), liquidity Rs 2 crore, 126-session gap per name, entry at next adjusted open, 35% trail, 756-session cap, cost 1.3%. Outcome A: close at least 3x entry within 756 sessions. Features, selection rule, tercile rule, pass bar as in Part 2 of this document (lift >= 1.4 overall, >= 1.15 in each volatility tercile, excess above the all-event mean on the first half, at most three, scored once on the second half with lift >= 1.5, n >= 150, excess above event mean and 0, at least half of years positive). Samples: events 2011-02..2018-12 (selection) and 2019-01..2023-11 (scored; full 756-session outcome windows).
