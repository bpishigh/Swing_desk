# VALUE-1: "extremely valuable but cheap" — registered 19 Nov 2026, before any run
Owner: "add to this where we identify stocks that are extremely valuable but cheap to get; this should also come from historical trends." Paper only; not advice.

## Data and its limits
India only. Quarterly results from data/india/filings with broadcast times (as-of, no look-ahead), figures through src/engine/fundamentals-asof.ts exactly as the nightly table builds them; market cap = RAW close on the day (not the back-adjusted close; see GROWTH-FACTORS-PREREG addendum RAWPRICE) times the share count of the filing, with splits dated on or before the day only. Month-ends 2017-07 to 2023-11 (four quarters of results needed; 756-session outcome window). Liquid group: 20-session traded value >= Rs 2 crore, raw price >= Rs 20.
Limits, stated now: (1) the filings list covers names still listed more completely than names that later vanished (see BATCH-A-REPLAY labels), so cheap-and-failed companies are under-represented and every value result is an upper bound; the evidence read is lift against the covered-stock rate, not a level. (2) Balance sheets only exist from Sep 2022, so no price-to-book, no net-debt enterprise value and no return on capital in this run. (3) The US cannot be done: its bars are adjusted to today and carry no raw prices, so market cap would be wrong (the US nightly table blocks split-like names for the same reason).

## Factors (computed per stock per month-end, non-null only; quintile cut points inside the covered stocks of that month-end)
EY earnings yield (TTM profit after tax / market cap); SY sales yield (TTM revenue / market cap); BY EBIT yield (TTM operating EBIT / market cap); GR revenue growth (TTM over TTM a year earlier); MG pre-exceptional profit margin (TTM pbtPre / TTM revenue); IC interest cover.
## Combinations (frozen now, nothing else is tried)
V1 cheap, growing, profitable: EY top fifth AND GR >= 10% AND MG at or above the covered median.
V2 cheap quality: EY top fifth AND EBIT margin top half AND IC >= 3 (non-financial only).
V3 growth at a low price: SY top fifth AND GR top half AND MG > 0.
## Outcomes
A: close at least 3x within 756 sessions; B: 5x; 12-month return (median and mean) minus the median of all liquid stocks that month ("excess").
## Bar (each half separately; halves: month-ends 2017-07..2020-12 and 2021-01..2023-11)
Combination: n >= 150 stock-months, A-rate lift >= 1.5 against the covered-stock rate, 12-month median above the covered median, mean excess > 0, and at least half the calendar years in the half with positive median excess. Single factors: reported only (top and bottom fifth lifts), no selection from them.
Pass: both halves meet the bar for the combination. A pass means "worth a paper book", read with the survivor limit above. The halves are not independent of 2020 (second half carries the rebound); the 2020 months are also reported on their own.

## Result (19 Nov 2026; one run over both halves, no amendments)
Coverage note found in the run: stock-months with usable as-of figures exist only from 2019-01 (24 month-ends in the first half = 2019 and 2020 only; earlier quarters lack broadcast times or full four-quarter chains), so the "first half" is 2019-2020 and the "second half" is 2021 to Nov 2023 (34 month-ends). Covered 3x rate 33.6% and 26.9%; 12-month median +34.7% and +19.0%.
Combinations against the bar: V1 (cheap, growing, profitable) n 31 in the first half and lift 0.82 in the second: fails. V2 (cheap quality) lift 1.03 and 0.99, median excess +2.6% and -0.5%: fails. V3 (low price to sales, growing, profitable) first half n 383 (all in 2020), lift 1.99, 12-month median +95.0% against +34.7%, excess +34.7 points; second half n 2,286, lift 1.49 (bar 1.5), 12-month median +29.2% against +19.0%, mean excess +31.7, median excess positive in 2021, 2022 and 2023 (+11.0, +5.5, +9.3). V3 misses the registered lift bar by 0.01 in the second half and its first-half evidence is one year, so by the rule it does not pass; it is the best of the three and I am not amending the bar.
Single factors (reported, no selection): top fifth of sales yield lift 1.75 and 1.44 with median excess +13.7 and +8.8 points, top fifth of EBIT yield 1.45 and 1.27 (+7.5 and +6.4), top fifth of earnings yield 1.39 and 1.20 (+5.8 and +6.0): cheapness on every measure beats the covered median in both halves, strongest for sales. The opposite fifths do not: high profit margin (lift 0.52 and 0.79, median excess -9.2 and -4.3), high interest cover (0.74, 0.72), expensive on sales (0.43, 0.57) all lose; high revenue growth alone does nothing (0.94, 0.94). The 2020 months on their own repeat the same ordering (sales yield 1.68, high margin 0.53).
Reading: in India the cheap end (low price to sales, to EBIT, to earnings) has beaten the market in 2019-2023 and the expensive, high-margin "quality" end has lagged; cheap alone is not enough to pass the strict combination bar once growth and profitability gates are added, and combinations that require high margin or high interest cover (V2) throw away the cheap winners. Cautions: the filings list is more complete for survivors than for names that vanished, so cheap-and-failed companies are under-represented and these lifts are upper bounds; 2019-2023 is one cycle and the 2020 rebound carries the first half; there are no balance sheets before Sep 2022, so real "asset value" (price to book, net cash, enterprise value) could not be tested. The existing Value-lite paper book (A2) already runs a cheap-stock rule; this result supports its direction and does not justify a new book.

## Addendum ANATOMY (registered 19 Nov 2026, before the run): the winners first, then what they had in common
Owner: "do this the same way we did for multibaggers: identify the stocks that won first and see the common things." Same India data and as-of figures as above (raw-price market cap, filings from 2019).
Part 1 (descriptive, no selection): the 460 India 4x winners from the multibagger anatomy (trough to peak within 1,008 sessions, saved set) whose trough falls in 2019-01..2023-11 and that have as-of figures. At the trough and at their first 252-day-high breakout: sales yield, EBIT yield, earnings yield, revenue growth, profit margin, interest cover and the percentile of each against the covered stocks of the previous month-end; share profitable; and how many were in the cheapest fifth on sales, EBIT or earnings yield ("cheap on any"). The same table for the non-winners that were cheap on any measure on the same month-ends (the comparison group).
Part 2 (tests): population = stock-months cheap on any measure (top fifth of SY, BY or EY at that month-end, month-ends 2019-01..2023-11 with a full 756-session outcome). Outcome A: 3x within 756 sessions; also 12-month excess against the median of all liquid stocks. Features at the month-end: revenue growth, profit margin, EBIT margin, interest cover, logTV, drawdown from the 2-year high, 6-month return, 126-session volatility, traded-value trend, and depth of cheapness (the stock's own sales yield percentile). Selection on the first period (month-ends 2019-01..2021-06) and scoring once on the second (2021-07..2023-11): a feature's top or bottom fifth is kept if its lift in A inside the cheap population is >= 1.4, >= 1.15 in each volatility tercile of that population, and its median 12-month excess is above the population's; at most three kept (largest smaller-tercile lift first); the combination is frozen with first-period cuts and passes if on the second period n >= 150, lift >= 1.5, median excess above the population median, mean excess > 0, and at least half the years positive. This split differs from the earlier 2019-20 / 2021-23 halves on purpose (the earlier first half was a single year).

### ANATOMY result (19 Nov 2026; the first execution stopped on a missing helper after Part 1 and before any Part 2 number was printed, the helper was added and the same code run once more; no rule changed)
Part 1. 333 of the 460 India 4x winners have their trough in 2019-01..2023-11; 165 have as-of figures at the trough. At the trough (medians): percentile of sales yield 76 (cheap), EBIT yield 73, earnings yield 76; revenue growth 6% (44th percentile), profit margin 8% (36th), interest cover at the 37th percentile; 87% profitable; 59% in the cheapest fifth on at least one measure. The 10x subset (55): sales yield at the 85th percentile (1.53x revenue market cap), growth 2%, margin 8%, 62% cheap on any. Crash launches (89): the cheapest (sales yield 86th percentile, 2.2 times revenue, 70% cheap on any), growth 5%; no-crash winners (42): less cheap (62nd percentile on sales, 77th on earnings), growth +15%, margin 10%. At the first breakout (148) they are no longer cheap (sales yield 62nd percentile, growth -1%, 39% cheap on any): by the time price breaks out the cheapness has been spent.
Cheap-on-any stock-months 2019-2023: those that became 3x (3,764) against those that did not (6,146): the same cheapness (sales-yield percentile 86 and 82), but lower profit margin (6% against 10%), lower interest cover (2.7 against 4.0), slightly lower revenue growth (15% against 19%), smaller traded value (log 18.1 against 18.4), slightly deeper drawdown from the 2-year high (-25% against -22%). The value winners are the cheap, thin-margin, more leveraged, smaller companies: turnarounds and operating-leverage stories rather than quality.
Part 2. Nothing kept: in the first period (2019-01..2021-06) no feature reached 1.4 within the cheap population with the tercile test (best: thin margin 1.16, small size 1.23, low interest cover 1.13, depth of cheapness 1.18); in the second period (2021-07..2023-11) the same features lift 1.07-1.22 (depth of cheapness 1.22, thin margin 1.16, low interest cover 1.12) and high margin, high EBIT margin, high interest cover and large size lose (0.58-0.94). So the direction is consistent in both periods but too weak to select on, and no combination was scored.
Reading: multibaggers in India are found among cheap, low-margin, leveraged, small names, but those traits only slightly improve the odds among cheap stocks as a whole; cheapness itself is the work (VALUE-1 single factors), and the extra traits are a tilt, not a screen. Same survivor and one-cycle cautions as VALUE-1; the 2020 trough accounts for most winners with figures.

## Addendum RATIOS (registered 19 Nov 2026, before the run): the ratios not yet tried
Owner asked whether P/E and other ratios were looked at. P/E (as earnings yield), price to sales and EBIT yield were. Added now, same data and as-of rules, nothing else tried:
Set A (month-ends 2019-01..2023-11 with 756-session outcomes; same halves as VALUE-1: 2019-2020 and 2021-2023; outcome A 3x and 12-month excess): PEG = price/earnings divided by TTM profit growth in percent (only for positive earnings and positive growth; low PEG = bottom fifth); NEY normalised earnings yield = average of the latest three fiscal-year profits after tax divided by market cap (top fifth; needs three fiscal years of figures); DY dividend yield = per-share dividends with ex-date in the previous 365 days (parsed from the corporate-actions subject text, rupee amounts only) divided by the raw close (top fifth, paying names only, and "pays nothing" reported as its own line).
Set B (balance-sheet ratios, month-ends 2022-09..latest with a 252-session outcome; the 3x outcome does not exist for these months, so the outcome is the 12-month excess and the share of names up 50% or more, "A12"): PB price to book (bottom fifth), EVE enterprise value to EBIT (bottom fifth, EV from the filings' net debt; non-financial), ROCE return on capital (top fifth), DE debt to equity (bottom fifth, non-financial); combinations frozen now: V4 = PB bottom fifth AND ROCE at or above median AND DE at or below median; V5 = EVE bottom fifth AND ROCE top fifth (the Magic Formula idea).
Bar: Set A single factors are reported with lift and years, no pass test (VALUE-1 combination bar unchanged and already settled). Set B is labelled thin evidence (about 38 month-ends in one market phase, overlapping 12-month windows); a rule is called "a lead" only if its A12 lift is >= 1.3 and its median excess > 0 in each of the two calendar years it covers.
