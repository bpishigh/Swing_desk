# GROUPS-1: does industry strength and market phase explain the best trades? — registered 20 Nov 2026, before the run
Follows STRATEGY-WINNERS (C-264): the best trades inside M1, M3 and the growth combo came in waves and in a few industries. No historical industry map exists; this uses a current-classification proxy and says so.
## Industry proxy
NSE broad industry for each name: the 501-name list in data/india/segments.json, else the inferred map (Yahoo industry mapped to NSE industry, data/india/industry-inferred.json + probe-industry-yf.json). Applied to all dates (industries rarely change). Names with no classification, including most names that later delisted, are left out of this test; coverage by year is reported, and results are read as direction only (survivor-leaning).
## Group state at each month-end (known at the signal)
Strength = median 6-month return of the group's liquid members (Rs 2 crore, raw price >= Rs 20, at least 8 liquid members, else the group is not scored that month); strength rank = percentile among scored groups. Breadth = share of those members above their own 200-session mean. A signal uses the state of the last month-end on or before it.
## Events and outcomes
M1, M3 and growth combo (GC) trades exactly as in STRATEGY-WINNERS (signals 2011-02..2023-11, 35% trail, 780 cap, costs 1.3%, 1.3%, 2.0%), with a known group and state. Outcome: net trade return minus Nifty 500 over the same dates ("excess").
## Tests (two groups of events: strong = strength rank >= 75th percentile, and weak = the rest; one run per half: 2011-02..2017-12 and 2018-01..2023-11)
G1 strong against weak: pass for a strategy if strong events beat weak events by >= +5 points of mean excess in both halves, n >= 150 strong events per half, mean excess excluding the best two trades still positive for strong, and strong beats weak in at least half the calendar years of each half. G2 same with breadth >= 60% as the strong side. G3 (market phase): events grouped by whether the share of groups with positive 6-month median return is above 70% at entry versus not; reported, same bar. A pass means "worth a group-strength gate in a registered book test", not a book. Reported for every cell regardless: n, mean and median net return, winners, excess, ex-best-2, by year.

## Results (one run; raw output in docs/replay/GROUPS-1-output.txt)
Fixes before the run, disclosed: the first launch stopped on a duplicate variable (no results printed); I removed the duplicate and let month-end states start from session 130 so early signals have a group state. Same rules, one real run. Signals start 2012 (the shared feature code needs 504 sessions of history), so the "2011-17" half is 2012-17.
Coverage: a scored group exists for 61% of M1 trades, 49% of M3, 59% of M4 and 40% of GC (delisted names have no industry). Survivor-leaning, direction only.
Verdict: 11 of 12 cells FAIL.
- G1 group strength (top quartile of industries by 6-month median return): no effect. M1 +1.8 / -7.1 points, M3 +2.1 / +0.8, GC -2.6 / -8.0 (strong minus rest, 2012-17 / 2018-23). Strong industries do not produce better new-high trades; M1 and GC were slightly worse in the second half.
- G2 breadth (>= 60% of members above their 200-day mean): sign flips between halves. M1 -1.9 then +24.2, M3 -1.6 then +16.4. It helped only in 2018-23, which is one market regime (2020-23) and is not a rule.
- G3 market phase (>70% of industries up): M1 -11.0 then +16.5, M3 -22.9 then +2.6, so it reverses in the first half. GC +22.0 / +16.5 passes the letter of the bar, but with only 2 and 4 scoreable years, the median in 2012-17 is worse for the strong side (-10.6% vs -4.8%), and the mean is carried by a few trades. Treated as noise, not a finding. No book built.
Conclusion: the industry and market-phase concentration seen in the best trades is a consequence of those trades happening together in time (2020-21 style waves), not a filter you can know in advance. The current books keep their rules unchanged.
