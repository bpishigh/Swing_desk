## Demergers without a typed record (C-50, proposed 15 Oct 2026)
The filing's cost share is the tax split of a purchase price, not the market's split, and often differs from the ex-date price fall (Allcargo, Veranda, DCM Shriram, Shankara). So a demerger with no usable typed record is now handled from the prices: a one-day fall of 15% or more is taken as the demerger and the earlier closes are scaled by the observed ratio ("adjusted from price"); a smaller fall drops the closes before the ex-date and the stock is treated like a recent listing. Typed records that pass the price check still win. Blocked-by-corporate-action stocks fell from 22 to 6 (the rest are bonus or NCRPS schemes). Status Proposed; criterion 2 (method check against typed records) could not be evaluated. Pending filing figures are kept in data/rules/demergers-pending.json for reference.

### L3. Filings sources: decision and probe findings (8 Nov 2026)
Owner decision: free route only. Probes ran on the GitHub runner on 8 Nov 2026 (five stocks per market, so completeness across the universe is not yet checked).

**India (NSE endpoints answer from the runner with no cookie warm-up)**
- Shareholding and pledge: 21 to 26 quarterly filings per stock, back to 2015 to 2021, with broadcast dates; pledge is in the linked XBRL file.
- Quarterly results: 47 to 158 filings per stock, back to 2007 (large names) or 2019 (KPIT), with filing dates; one market-wide call returned 2,906 filings for Jan to Mar 2021. The per-stock list stops about Jan 2025, so recent quarters come from the market-wide call.
- Insider trades (PIT): date-range queries work; 13,802 trades for Jan to Mar 2019 in one call.
- SAST: `corporate-sast-reg29` answers (4 to 20 filings per stock back to 2021); the other name tried gave 404.
- Announcements (results dates, buybacks): 640 to 3,375 per stock, Reliance back to 2004. Board meetings: about 20 per stock including upcoming. Corporate actions: returns oldest records only; the desk's own 2019 history stays the source. The pledge-only endpoint returns empty; BSE API refuses (403).

**US**
- SEC EDGAR (needs the SEC_UA contact): company facts carry `filed`, `form`, `fy`, `fp` per value, back to 2007 to 2009 (2015 for CELH); Form 4 lists back to 2015 to 2020 with transaction XML; 13D/G and 13F (Berkshire 39 filings to 2017) in the same lists; filing times present.
- Cautions: concept tags change over time (revenue moves between tags), and each fact repeats for every filing that reported it, so an as-of view takes the earliest `filed` per period.
- FINRA short interest: pipe-delimited files download, including 2019. Nasdaq earnings calendar answers (EPS forecast, fiscal quarter; surprise not supplied).

**yfinance (version 1.8.0)**
- `get_earnings_dates`: estimate, reported EPS, surprise %; back to 2002 to 2008 for large names, 2010 to 2019 for others; uneven (JPPOWER stops at 2015). Used for the earnings-surprise number only.
- Quarterly statements only 5 to 7 quarters; insider transactions about two years (US) and mostly empty for India; holders are snapshots; nothing for delisted names (SIVB empty).

**Completeness probe, round 2 (8 Nov 2026; 57 listed India stocks sampled every 20th by traded value, 8 stocks absent from today's market, 61 US stocks, 15 yfinance-checked absent names; `data/probe-filings-coverage.json`, `data/probe-yfinance-coverage.json`)**
- India results: quarters are 99% complete on average (minimum 92%) from the first filing (median 2013) to Dec 2024; 14 of 49 stocks have one or more missing quarters. Each quarter usually has a standalone and a consolidated filing (consolidated is 35% of records), so an as-of loader must keep both and choose one by a written rule. Every record has an XBRL link, but only 46 of 46 newest files, 27 of 49 mid-history files and 21 of 49 oldest files held the revenue and profit tags I looked for (the rest are 404 or lack those tags): **results history before about 2020 is patchy in structured form.**
- **India results from Q4 FY25 onward (found, 8 Nov 2026):** the old results endpoint covers filings up to the March 2025 filing window (about 3,865 filings), then almost nothing, because results moved to SEBI's integrated filing. The new endpoint is `/api/integrated-filing-results?index=equities&type=Integrated Filing- Financials` with `symbol`, `period_ended` (for example 30-Jun-2025), `page` and `size` (size up to 5,000 answered). It returns 3,190 to 4,996 filings per quarter end from 31-Mar-2025 to 30-Sep-2026 (2,172 to 2,366 symbols; Sep 2026 partial at 1,701), and 0 for the 31-Dec-2024 quarter, which the old endpoint covers, so the two endpoints join without a gap. Each record has `broadcast_Date`, `qe_Date`, Standalone or Consolidated, `type_Sub` (Original or revised), `revised_Date` and an XBRL link. Of 33 sampled stocks, 30 returned filings (the three without are renamed or merged names). The XBRL files (served as XML) carry revenue from operations, profit before tax and profit for the period under `in-capmkt` tags with the unit in rupees (rounded to a crore or less); banks use different tags (for example `RevenueOnInvestments`, `ProfitLossForThePeriod`), so the loader needs a tag map per format (Ind AS, banking). I did not check which period (quarter or cumulative) the `OneD` context holds, or EPS tags.
- **Results dates as a fallback:** the announcements endpoint carries results-like announcements (outcome of board meeting, financial results) with timestamps through Nov 2026 for all 20 stocks sampled (9 to 28 since Feb 2025 each).
- India shareholding: 100% of quarters present from Sep 2021 to Sep 2026 for listed stocks (the per-stock list starts in 2021); 133 revised filings were seen, so the loader keeps both the original and the revision date. Pledge and percentage tags were found in 166 of 170 files checked.
- India PIT: market-wide yearly calls return 3,729 (2015) to 58,542 (2018) trades and 13,545 (2025); no cap observed at year size. Whole history from 2016 is usable.
- Stocks absent from today's market: 3 of 8 had a results list (to 2022 on median) and 2 of 8 had shareholding. Delisting history is partial and not survivorship-free.
- US (EDGAR): 59 of 61 sampled had company facts (one no CIK, one 404). Net income, assets, equity and operating cash flow present for 53, 53 and 52 of 59 and revenue for 49 of 59 (diluted EPS 48), with a median 15 years of filings (from about 2011). Revenue and equity sit under several tags (36 of 49 and 33 of 53 needed more than one), so the loader needs a tag fallback chain. Form 4: the recent list reaches back to a median of Feb 2020, with older pages for 34 of 60 stocks.
- US delisted filers: SVB, Bed Bath and Beyond and CIT still have filings and company facts on EDGAR; First Republic and Signature have filings but no company facts (404). So EDGAR keeps delisted history, but the ticker list does not, and the loader needs CIKs from another source.
- FINRA short interest: files found for Jun and Dec of 2018, 2020, 2022 and 2024; none found for 2008 to 2016 under the file-name pattern tried, so history is from about 2018 (unconfirmed for earlier years).
- yfinance earnings surprise: US 55 of 61 stocks have at least eight reported quarters with estimates (median first date Aug 2002); India 41 of 65, 15 empty (including the absent names), median first date Nov 2015. Use for large and mid caps; do not depend on it for small caps.

**Close-out (probe round 4, 8 Nov 2026):** the `OneD` context is the quarter and `FourD` the year to date; EPS tags exist (Ind AS: `BasicEarningsLossPerShareFromContinuingAndDiscontinuedOperations`; banking: `...AfterExtraordinaryItems`). Results XBRL exists from about 2019 (`in-bse-fin` tags) and returns 404 for 2010 to 2016, so structured India fundamentals before 2019 are not available free. FINRA's earliest file found is 29 Dec 2017 (earlier dates refused; the API returned nothing for 2012 and 2015). SEC's `cik-lookup-data.txt` maps names to CIKs for every filer ever, but no ticker-to-CIK history exists, so one must be built for delisted US filers.
