STEP 5 · Alerts (your final message is the phone notification: short, plain, at most 8 lines). Rules version R-2026.10.5: the regime below is the same one the Swing Desk app and the Engine Rules document use.
- Regime for D and for the previous tracker doc from tracker docs up to that date (if a doc lacks sma50/sma200 use the latest earlier values): Trend per index = above both 50 and 200 SMA with the 50 SMA higher than in the previous doc that has one → Green; below both → Red; else Yellow; take the weaker index. Distribution day = non-skip session down 0.2% or more on higher volume than the previous NON-SKIPPED session (a skipped day's volume is never the comparison), counted over the last 25 sessions (weekdays), dropping any followed by a close 5% or more above it; 5 or more caps at Yellow. Latest breadth (last 3 docs) below 40 caps at Yellow. Follow-through day (Engine Rules C-35): look for it only inside the current correction. The correction is armed when the close is 8% or more below the highest close of the last 60 sessions, or when the trend reads Red (below both SMAs), whichever comes first, and it is disarmed again once the close goes above that 60-session high. When it arms, the correction low is the lowest low since that 60-session closing high (never the lowest low of the whole stored series); day 1 = the first close above the previous day's high after that low; on day 4 or later a close up 1.25% or more on higher volume than the previous non-skipped session is a follow-through day. It lifts Red to Yellow only, never to Green. It fails on a close below the rally low, or if breadth has not passed 50% within 10 sessions; a new low after arming restarts the count. If the regime changed, lead with it.
- New distribution or follow-through day: one line.
- Open trades (R per share = lots[0].price − stop0; shares = lots − sells): close at or below stop; +1R and not added; +2R and third not sold; close below the 20 EMA after the third is sold (if that stock has emaApprox:true, say "20 EMA approximate, confirm on Dhan"); resultsDate within 2 trading days; 10+ weekdays since entryDate without maxClose reaching +1R.
- Legacy "pass" docs: close at or below stop, or below the 200 SMA.
- One line naming anything still missing, only if something is.
If nothing to report, end with exactly: "Swing Desk updated for <D>: no alerts."
