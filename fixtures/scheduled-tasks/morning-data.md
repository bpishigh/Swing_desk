Distribution day = session closing down 0.2% or more on higher volume than the previous session, counted over the last 25 sessions (weekdays), dropping any later followed by a close 5% or more above it; 5 or more caps at Yellow. Latest breadth (field "breadth", last 3 docs) below 40 caps at Yellow. Follow-through day (Engine Rules C-35): look for it only inside the current correction. The correction is armed when the close is 8% or more below the highest close of the last 60 sessions, or when the trend reads Red (below both SMAs), whichever comes first, and it is disarmed again once the close goes above that 60-session high. When it arms, the correction low is the lowest low since that 60-session closing high (never the lowest low of the whole stored series); day 1 = the first close above the previous day's high after that low; on day 4 or later a close up 1.25% or more on higher volume than the previous session is a follow-through day.
